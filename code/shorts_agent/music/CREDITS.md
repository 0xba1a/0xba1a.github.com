- **No attribution required.** Free for commercial use, including monetized YouTube
  videos. Mixing, looping, trimming, and fading are all permitted (no
  "NoDerivatives" restriction).

These are low-key, upbeat instrumental cues chosen to sit quietly under narration.
The builder (`build_short.py`) rotates through them — each short gets one track,
picked deterministically from its slug, looped/trimmed to length, faded in/out, and
mixed well under the voice (default ≈ −26 dB).

Although no credit is legally required, a courtesy line such as
"Music: David Zorff (public domain)" in the video description is appreciated.
