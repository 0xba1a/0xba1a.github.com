hardware bugs are bad. But software bugs are catastropic.

If a poorly manufactured hard-disk will have more failure rate. It will randomly fail during operation which will be annoying.

But software failures will look like coordianted attack. On June 30, 2012, a leap second adjustment which wasn't handled properly in the Linux kernel brought half of the Internet down.

On an another instance, an integer overrun bug in a firmware of an SSD crashed the SSD and made the content unreadable exactly after 32768 hours of operation. Imagine the storage of your entire datacetner coming down at a single second and wipe all your customer data.

So, proper design and fault ingestion testing is crucial
