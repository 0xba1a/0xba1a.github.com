Google engineers start randomly killing their microservices, pull out the network cables, switch off tor switches or unplug an entire data center.

They are not hacking google. But they testing how tolerant their systems are. It is called fault injestion. A service or a component failure is a fault. If it brings your entire system down, it is failure. You can develop a faulty component. But as an engineer you shouldn't let your system fail.

But how will you know whether you have designed a fault tolerant system? only by injesting the fault. That's why they purposefully harming their system and see how it behaves and manages the fault.

It is called chaos engineering.
